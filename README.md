# Vrinda Earth Naturals 🌿
### 100% Organic Luxury Ayurvedic Skincare Store & Admin Portal

Vrinda Earth Naturals is an authentic Indian manufacturer of pure, handcrafted Ayurvedic skincare essentials.

## ✨ Features
- **Royal Aesthetic & Branding:** Warm sand, ivory, antique gold, and deep earth walnut palette derived directly from the brand logo and packaging.
- **Hero Skincare Lineup:**
  - Herbal Face Wash Powder (Hero Cleanser)
  - Steam-Distilled Magic Rose Water
  - Magic Activator Elixir
  - The Royal Radiant Glow Trio Combo (3-in-1 Regimen)
- **Interactive Daily Ritual Guide:** The 3-Step Ayurvedic daily skincare regimen with pro-tips.
- **Purity Comparison:** Comprehensive analysis contrasting ordinary foaming chemical cleansers with 100% botanical formulations.
- **Full E-Commerce Cart & Ordering:**
  - Slide-out Cart Drawer with dynamic pricing and free shipping progress meter.
  - Multi-option Checkout (Cash On Delivery, UPI QR Code, and Direct WhatsApp Order).
  - Instant Order Confirmation receipt generation.
- **Integrated Admin Control Panel (`admin.html`):**
  - **PIN Security:** Default passcode `1234`
  - **Product & MRP Manager:** Edit prices, MRP, discounts, images (with computer upload support), ingredients, and usage instructions.
  - **Customer Orders Tracker:** Real-time view of customer orders, status manager (Pending ➔ Confirmed ➔ Dispatched ➔ Delivered), print invoices, and direct WhatsApp customer messaging.
  - **Store Content Customizer:** Edit announcement bar, hero section headlines, WhatsApp numbers, shipping thresholds, and founders' details.
  - **Coupons & Discounts:** Create custom discount codes.
  - **Backup & Restore:** Full JSON export and restore capabilities.
